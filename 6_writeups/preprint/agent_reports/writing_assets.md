- Published (R/5_outputs/pre_pipeline/mirt_humanmerge_lineageprior_lineagebm/axis_names.json): Fluid Intelligence; Scientific Knowledge and Reasoning; Agentic Capabilities; Legacy QA.
- 2026-10-19 no-ceiling fit, mode A: Fluid Intelligence & Formal Sciences; Agentic & Cyber Capabilities; Scientific Knowledge & Reasoning; Visual & Commonsense (this is what README shows).
- 2026-10-19 ceiling fit (current default, provisional, "Investigation 2026-10-21 (Claude, provisional)"): Fluid Intelligence & Formal Sciences; Agentic Capabilities & Programming; Cyber Capabilities; Visual & Commonsense. There is no science axis in its primary frame; its secondary_mode keeps the no-ceiling names.
- Sciences Po deck (five-chain refit): Software, tools and research; Cybersecurity and interactive environments; Mathematical and scientific reasoning.
- Rule: axes carry no names in code; named by hand from top benchmarks by axis share ("loading rows collinear with the axis"); a name holds only while one of its 2-3 signature benchmarks stays in the axis's top list; post scripts refuse unconfirmed names. Each name is derived from the loadings in one sentence.

Other locks:
- "Frontier points", not "records", for the top-2 reading (commit f1915d1, 2026-10-20, frontier-gap labels and French i18n).
- ECI-H = ECI with Human baselines, distinct from Epoch's published ECI; anchors Claude 3.5 Sonnet (2024-10-22) = 130, GPT-5 (2025-08-07, medium) = 150, per draw. Never overlay ECI-H on Epoch's ECI as if one scale.
- No pre-2023 crossover dates: a passed tier shows "passed, p = X" (or an arrow at the window edge), never a backdated year; axis-2 "2022" was a backward extrapolation (first WMDP/GPQA machine scores 2023-06). Note: R/6_writeups/blogpost/figures/make_crossover.py still uses a 2015-2030 window and prints clipped true dates in small type, so the code does not enforce this lock.
- Forecast and figure conventions: records read off posterior medians; legacy QA axis excluded from forecasts; charts end at 2030; human tiers drawn only where measured; ability axes in human units (Average Human 0, Top Performer 1, `config.ABILITY_SCALE = "human"`) on ability-side figures; README text says "fitted scale (models' mean at 0)", a conflict to settle.
- Methods first, then results; positive loadings from the start (signed/rotation story, horseshoe and PPCA cut to an appendix); the LOO ladder (one assumption per step, same scope, same floors) is the core methods evidence; both modes reported, main line on one, the other in an appendix; Pareto-k breakdown beside every elpd.
- Style (CLAUDE.md/AGENTS.md style guide and memory): short declarative sentences, claim then evidence; ASD-STE100 for dense passages; no em dashes; no colon constructions in prose; no rhetorical questions, no teaser sentences, no storytelling; banned words "delve / crucially / notably / it is worth noting / comprehensive", no "not just X but Y"; define a term once; every count with its denominator ("78 / 200,000 divergences"); effect sizes against posterior SD; claim strength matches evidence ("explored" is not "tested"); convergence readout = min/median bulk ESS plus divergences, r-hat secondary and only on identified quantities; final result first, failures and history to an appendix; audience a statistician, no file names or run bookkeeping; past fits get no numbers; figures self-explanatory from title and legend, every line in the legend, no text blocks on canvas, English axis text, colorblind-safe palette, inspect the render; titles terse noun labels; per-paragraph consent when editing Yassine's hand-revised text; LW LLM policy (Yassine writes the post prose; for the paper confirm the authorship rule with him).

## 4. Public claims the paper must change, retract or explain

1. Humans fitted inside the K=4 model as full test-takers (post TL;DR, Methods, Fig. 2). Main now fits models only and places tiers per axis afterwards, because jointly fitted tiers traded about -5 sd on one axis against +8 on another. The canonical K=1 ECI-H still fits them inside. The paper must state the change and its reason.
2. The four axes and their names. Fluid Intelligence, Scientific Knowledge and Reasoning, Agentic Capabilities, Legacy QA do not survive the 2026-10-19 data: agentic merges with cyber or splits from it by mode, a Visual & Commonsense axis appears, Legacy QA disappears, and in the ceiling fit no science axis remains in the primary mode.
3. "Scientific Knowledge: AI has passed all nine tiers, p 0.85 to 0.95; every model above humans since 2023." Depends on the joint fit and on an axis that no longer exists in the ceiling fit. Retract or re-derive.
4. Crossover dates: Fluid Domain Expert by spring 2027, Top Performer by mid-2028; Agentic Skilled Generalist now, Committee of Domain Experts by mid-2027; 95% windows close by 2035. Forecast method changed (record envelope, SD < 0.33, 18-month window, then two-regime top-2 lines fitted on medians from the first reasoning release). All dates must be recomputed and the change explained.
5. Convergence and mode claims. Post: one solution (Annex B "1 mode"), "four of ten chains" in a second mode (French: settles on all chains; LW: most runs). Main: 20 chains, two modes (17 / 3, 8.7 nats). The 2026-10-21 five-chain refit deliverable records failed convergence (three loading solutions). The paper needs one honest statement for the fit it reports.
6. Annex B LOO ladder. The rungs were fitted on a 5,004-obs / 98-benchmark scope (ladder obs_check.json) while the published flagship saw 4,923 / 96; the final row prints 10 x 12,000 and 37 divergences (published trace) beside an elpd 7,710.4 +- 76.3 that matches the 10 x 20,000 reference fit (78 / 200,000 divergences, 6_writeups/blogpost/ladder/comparison.csv). Rerun the ladder on one G3 scope.
7. "Only a chance floor is set; ceilings future work." Ceilings are now estimated by default (+56 +- 12 nats robust LOO at equal axis structure).
8. Calibration: post PIT variance 0.046 (in-sample, n = 4,849). Main reports LOO-PIT variance 0.067 at K=4. Replace and say the metric changed.
9. Likelihood: post describes Beta noise on every score; main uses Beta-binomial on item-scored benchmarks. The French page's formula sigma(4 sum A(theta - D)) does not match the code's sum A theta - D.
10. Abilities: post used a Gaussian zero-sum block; main uses a skew-normal block; the ECI-H 1D still Gaussian.
11. Scope numbers: post 97 benchmarks (list) / 4,923 obs / 829 takers; main 107 benchmarks / 5,487 obs / 887 models (+57 human scores placed).
12. The Barry memo's "axis 2 passed the top performer in 2022" was never published but is in the 08-24 memo; it is a backward extrapolation and must not reappear.
